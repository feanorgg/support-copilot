# How to Open an Account

Source: https://valetax.com/how-to-open-an-account/ (snapshot 2026-10-26)

Discover a seamless trading experience with us. Open your account with us in 3 quick and simple steps

## Steps

1. Visit www.valetax.com and click on ‘ Create Account ‘ at the top right of the page
2. Enter your details in the required fields and proceed to create your account
3. Upon successful registration, you will be provided access to your own Member Area
4. Once completed, you’re ready to make your first deposit . Visit How to Deposit for a quick guide to start trading with us!

## Got a question on Opening an account?

### How do I sign up for an account?

Click here follow the step-by-step instructions to verify and fund your account to start trading. Opening a live account allows you to begin trading immediately. We recommend clients to read through our Risk Disclosures, Privacy Policy and Terms of Business before they start trading.

### What account type should I open?

It will depend on your trading strategy and preferred trading instruments. You may view the various account types here . You may open another account type that suits you better at a later stage too.

### What is the minimum initial deposit needed to open an account?

It will depend on the account type that is being selected. You may view details of all our accounts here and the minimum deposit that is required for each account type.

### Do I need to provide any documents to open an account?

For live accounts, we will require a Proof of identification and Proof of address via a bank statement or a utility bill that are not older than 6 months. Please ensure that your name is clearly displayed in these documents.

### How long does it take to open an account?

Account opening and document verification will be completed within 48 hours.

### What leverage settings should I select?

Your leverage setting is dependent on your trading strategy and goals. At Valetax, we offer up to 1:2000 depending on account types. For more details, visit our Account types page here for more details.

### Do you offer an Islamic-friendly account?

All our account are Islamic friendly and there is no swap is credited or withdrawn from all trading account.
